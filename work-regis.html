Registration++

Time:
Three months.
Collaboration:
Member of a four person group.
Tools:
Figma, Illustrator, Google Suite, Excel.

Problem

Undergraduates often face uncertainty and frustration during course registration due to scheduling conflicts, varying course difficulty, and uneven workload distribution.

Solution

A “one stop shop” for all things UT registration: the course catalog, class descriptions, stats and syllabi, grade curves, RateMyProfessor reviews, and a schedule creator all in one app. This tool addresses these challenges by helping students create well-balanced schedules that reduce conflicts, align with their academic goals, and provide greater confidence in the registration process.

Reflections

The end to end experience - This project was an experience of the entire UX research and design process in a microcosm, from user research to building design systems to iteration, all while making and defending design decisions with my team.

Where students really build their schedules - As we discovered from our user interviews, the integrated UT schedule builder doesn’t serve many students. Students look for schedule-building functionality in apps like Excel rather than looking internally. By consolidating the entire registration process into one tab, more time would be spent on the application rather than bouncing between different solutions.
